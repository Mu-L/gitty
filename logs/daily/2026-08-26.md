## Why it matters

**Why do this?**

Two versions of one file is the comparison a history browser is opened for, and
the history pane could not make it: it listed the revisions and then spent a
single click leaving them.

**What problem does it solve?**

The pane had click semantics of its own — one click opens — which nothing else
in the window shares, and no way to say "this version against that one". Both
are now the commit log's, so the two lists are not learned separately.

**What does it affect?**

`FileHistoryPane` holds the picks, `RepoTab.showFileRange` opens the range with
the file selected, and `FileDoc` carries the callback between them. The rule is
written down in `ref/spec/renderer.md` under the `View` union, and in the manual
beside blame.
