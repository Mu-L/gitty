## Why it matters

**Why do this?**

The day's largest piece was not a feature at all: an interface that wandered a
pixel or two for as long as the window was open, on nothing more exotic than a
laptop with a second screen. Around it, four items are about the repository
being a place you work rather than a thing you read — files pasted in from the
desktop, a program from an old commit actually run, a commit opened on the site
that hosts it, a submodule pulled without leaving for the terminal.

**What problem does it solve?**

The shaking was measured rather than guessed at, and the measurements ruled out
everything except one Chromium feature — which is why the fix is a relaunch
narrowed to the case that needs it, not a switch thrown for everyone. The paste
closes the last gap where the terminal was the only way to get a file into the
tree. Running a snapshot's program was shipped and then fixed the same night:
files are not a repository, and a work tree is what the scripts turned out to
need.

**What does it affect?**

`clipfiles.ts` joins the pure modules with its own test file, so the clipboard
shapes a desktop uses are pinned by fixtures rather than rediscovered. The
scale relaunch, its three guards and the clamp that was tried and dropped are
in `ref/spec/main-process.md`, and the manual says what to do for a monitor
plugged in after launch — the one case the relaunch deliberately does not
catch, since restarting would take the terminal panes' shells with it. 0.1.9
ships all of it.
