a page the site would not have until the work was pushed.

**What problem does it solve?**

accelerator was no fallback: one fires only while the compositor calls the
window focused, which under Wayland it frequently does not. A hash the remote
cannot know is a 404, not a guess worth making.

`RepoTab` and `contextMenus.ts`; both specs and the manual.
